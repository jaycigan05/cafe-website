SAIBC (Sunway AI Builders Club)

Head of Public Relations

SAIBC (Sunway AI Builders Club) brings together AI builders from across the university. While there are already several tech clubs on campus, SAIBC focuses specifically on artificial intelligence — running workshops, post-project sharing sessions, and related activities, with plans to host a hackathon in the future.

We have successfully organised our first few events, and for our upcoming workshop we have invited industry professionals to lead a hands-on AI session, teaching students and members how to build a chatbot, how to prompt effectively for ideal results, and how these skills connect to automation.

My main responsibilities include sourcing and coordinating the emcee, event photography, and online event promotion.
