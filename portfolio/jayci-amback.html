Ambank HackLab 2.0

AI Customer Experience (Web2)

At the AmBank Hackathon, my focus was on exploring Web2 technologies through a real-world business problem. My project centred on improving the AI-driven customer experience for a financial institution: I worked on an AI platform designed to help banking customers resolve their questions and problems more quickly — answering enquiries, guiding them through common issues, and reducing their need to wait for human support.

The experience gave me hands-on exposure to applying AI to practical, customer-facing challenges in the financial sector.
