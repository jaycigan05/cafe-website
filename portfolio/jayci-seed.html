SEED Programme (Scholars Empowering & Enriching Dreams)

2026 Cohort · Organised by Sunway Student Ambassadors (SSA)

The SEED Programme (Scholars Empowering & Enriching Dreams) 2026 is a curated leadership initiative by the Sunway Student Ambassadors (SSA), designed to help scholars grow beyond academics by building leadership skills while creating real impact in the community. Running from April to November 2026, it selects only six scholars for its pioneering batch.

As a participant, I plan and host themed workshops — such as STEM and Entrepreneurship sessions — under the SLP, and support MYReaders literacy sessions by coordinating volunteers and providing one-on-one English reading support for children at the Taman Megah Children Centre.

Beyond the leadership, project-management, and teamwork experience, the programme recognises scholars with volunteer hours, a certificate, and an appreciation dinner.
