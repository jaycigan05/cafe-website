Our story

A cafe where paws are always welcome

We started Pawfee Café because coffee tastes better with a wagging tail nearby. What began as a dream to blend specialty brews with animal rescue is now a cozy community hub where every latte pairs with unconditional love. Whether you are here to unplug with a furry companion or meet your future best friend, every sip supports a rescue pup's journey to a forever home. Stop by, grab your favorite drink and let us prove that happiness is just a pawprint away.
